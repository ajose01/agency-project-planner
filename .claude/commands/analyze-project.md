Walk me through a structured project intake analysis for a new client project.

Follow the framework in `analyze/project-intake.md` exactly. Do the following:

1. Ask me the questions from **Section 1** (Project Goal). Wait for my answers before continuing.

2. Ask me the questions from **Section 2** (Trigger) and **Section 3** (Output) together — present them as a checklist I can respond to.

3. Present the **Section 4 complexity axes table** and ask me to rate each axis 1–3. Help me by suggesting ratings based on what I've told you so far, and let me confirm or adjust.

4. Ask about **Section 5** (Timeline & Scope).

5. Based on my answers, calculate the complexity score and apply the **Section 6 recommendation guide**. Present:
   - The recommended stack with a clear reason
   - An alternative stack if applicable and why I might choose it instead
   - Any tradeoffs I should be aware of

6. Go through **Section 7** (Integration Inventory) — check off which patterns apply and surface the relevant pattern files I should reference.

7. Output a completed **Section 8 Project Brief** that I can save or hand off.

After the brief is complete, suggest running `/scaffold-project` with the chosen stack.

Throughout the conversation, be direct. Do not default to any particular tool. If n8n and a TypeScript script are genuinely equivalent for my use case, say so and explain the deciding factor.
